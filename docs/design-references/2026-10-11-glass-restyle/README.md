## Translation into KawTeam

- Palette. Teal #00545f stays the primary action color. Lime #d6fb00 and pale lime #ecffb6 stay as highlight and selection. Forest green and mint appear ONLY inside the background aurora gradients.
- Glass. Translucent white panels, backdrop blur plus saturate, a bright 1px top edge, faint inner glow, soft green tinted shadow. Think iOS 26 liquid glass on a light wallpaper.
- Type. Self-hosted TeX Gyre Heros (free Helvetica clone) for everything, large display-style scale, tight tracking on headings.
